# 💤 Sleep Health and Lifestyle Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Sleep Health and Lifestyle Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze sleep patterns, lifestyle habits, and health indicators. The dashboard transforms raw sleep health data into meaningful insights that can help identify relationships between sleep quality, occupation, physical activity, heart rate, BMI, and sleep disorders.

This project demonstrates my ability to:
- Clean and transform data using Power Query.
- Build data models in Power BI.
- Create interactive dashboards.
- Design meaningful visualizations.
- Generate actionable business and healthcare insights.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are to:

- Analyze sleep duration across different BMI categories.
- Investigate the relationship between daily steps and heart rate.
- Understand the distribution of sleep disorders.
- Compare average sleep duration across occupations.
- Build an interactive dashboard suitable for healthcare professionals and researchers.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Average Sleep Duration by BMI Category
**Chart Type:** Clustered Column Chart

This visualization compares the average sleep duration of individuals across different BMI categories.

**Key Insight**
- People with a Normal BMI generally record healthier sleep durations.
- Obese and Overweight categories tend to show lower average sleep duration.

---

### 2. Daily Steps vs Heart Rate
**Chart Type:** Scatter Plot

This chart explores the relationship between physical activity and resting heart rate.

**Key Insight**
- Individuals with higher daily step counts generally have lower resting heart rates.
- It helps identify active versus sedentary lifestyles.

---

### 3. Sleep Disorder Distribution
**Chart Type:** Pie Chart

Shows the proportion of people with:

- No Sleep Disorder
- Insomnia
- Sleep Apnea

**Key Insight**
- Most individuals have no diagnosed sleep disorder.
- Insomnia and Sleep Apnea make up smaller portions of the dataset.

---

### 4. Occupation vs Average Sleep Duration
**Chart Type:** Horizontal Bar Chart

Compares average sleep duration among different occupations.

**Key Insight**
- Certain occupations experience significantly less sleep than others.
- Work demands appear to influence sleep duration.
